Porsche 911 GT3

About the Car

The Porsche 911 GT3 is a German sports car with a powerful boxer engine, excellent handling and a sporty design.

Specifications

  • Engine: 4.0L Flat-Six
  • Power: 510 HP
  • Top Speed: 311 km/h (PDK)
  • 0–100 km/h: 3.4 seconds (PDK)
  • Drive: Rear-Wheel Drive
